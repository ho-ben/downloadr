Why the extra button?

Poe keeps chats behind a protected frame.

A normal PWA cannot see across that browser boundary—and should not ask you to upload session cookies. The bookmark runs only when you choose it on poe.com, uses Poe’s existing sign-in locally, and offers a file download that is not limited by Android’s clipboard size.
